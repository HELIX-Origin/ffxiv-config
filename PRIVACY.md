# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-19 · **Version 0.1.0**

**ffxiv-config** ("the App") is built around one principle: **your data stays on your machine.**
This policy explains what the App stores, what it transmits, and what it never collects.

## 📦 What the App stores (locally)

| Data | Storage Location | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App settings | Local application data directory | Storing user preferences and configuration |

- All app data is stored **only on your local machine**.
- Nothing is uploaded to any server run by the publisher.

## 🌐 What the App transmits

The application operates locally on your machine.

- The App contacts only the hosts required for its documented features:
  None by default.
- Credentials you provide (e.g. API keys) are sent **only** to the corresponding provider and are
  stored locally.

## 🚫 What the App never does

- No telemetry or analytics by default.
- No accounts created on any publisher server.
- No selling or sharing of personal data.
- No tracking across other applications or websites.

## 📬 Contact

Questions? Open an issue or discussion on the project's issue tracker.

## 🗑️ Removing your data

Uninstall the application and remove the local application data directory.

## 🔄 Changes

We may update this policy; the current version always lives in this repository as `PRIVACY.md`.
